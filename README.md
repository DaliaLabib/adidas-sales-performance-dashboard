# 📊 Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Excel sales analytics dashboard designed to monitor revenue, volume, and profit performance across Middle Eastern regional markets, retail channels, and product categories for Adidas.

## 🚀 Dataset Overview & Key Features

### 📊 Dataset Summary
- **Total Records:** 1,200 orders across 3 years (2023 – 2025).
- **Total Units Sold:** 3,550 units.
- **Total Revenue:** ~$287,378 USD.
- **Total Profit:** ~$85,070 USD.
- **Regions Tracked:** Egypt, Iraq, KSA (Saudi Arabia), Lebanon, Oman.
- **Sales Channels:** Online, Retail, Outlet, Wholesale.
- **Product Categories:** Apparel, Footwear, Accessories.

---

## 🔑 Key Features & Dashboard Interactive Slicers

- **KPI Header Bar:** High-level summary displaying Total Units Sold, Total Revenue, and Total Profit.
- **Dynamic Slicers (Filters):** Interactive side panel allowing users to slice sales data by:
  - **Order Date / Timeline:** Year-level breakdown (2023–2025).
  - **Region:** Specific country filtering across the Middle East.
  - **Category:** Accessories, Apparel, and Footwear.
  - **Store Type:** Online, Outlet, Retail, and Wholesale.
- **Sales Method Breakdown (Pie Chart):** Distribution of revenue across channels (Online leading at 46%, followed by Retail at 35%, Outlet at 12%, and Wholesale at 7%).
- **Regional Financial Performance (Bar Chart):** Revenue vs. Profit comparison by country.
- **Yearly Performance Trends:** Revenue and Profit comparative tracking across 2023, 2024, and 2025.
- **Product Profitability Breakdown:** Detailed profit analysis across 20+ specific product SKUs grouped by Footwear (*Predator Freak*, *Ultraboost Light*, *Copa Sense*), Apparel (*Primegreen Jacket*, *Z.N.E. Hoodie*), and Accessories (*Originals Backpack*).

---

## 📈 Executive Insights & Data Findings

1. **Top Sales Channel:** **Online** is the primary driver of revenue, accounting for **46%** of total sales, followed by **Retail (35%)**.
2. **Leading Regional Markets:** **Egypt** and **Iraq** lead in revenue generation and overall profit across the Middle Eastern territories.
3. **Top Performing Products:**
   - **Footwear:** *Predator Freak* ($9,218 profit) and *Ultraboost Light* ($8,876 profit).
   - **Apparel:** *Primegreen Jacket* ($7,687 profit) and *Z.N.E. Hoodie* ($7,070 profit).
   - **Accessories:** *Originals Backpack* ($3,355 profit).
